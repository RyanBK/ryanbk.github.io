Welcome!

I am an Assistant Professor of Political Science at the [University of Nevada, Reno](https://www.unr.edu/political-science){:target="_blank"}, where I teach courses in American politics and quantitative methods for undergraduates, MPA students, and PhD students. 

I received my Ph.D. in 2025 from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://polisci.ucla.edu/){:target="_blank"}. I spent the 2024-2025 academic year as a Predoctoral Fellow at the [Center for the Study of Democratic Institutions](https://www.vanderbilt.edu/csdi/){:target="_blank"} at Vanderbilt University.

I study how people learn about politics. In particular, my research explores how salient events interact with voters' partisan and social identities to shape elections and public opinion in the United States. I especially focus on voters who live closest to focusing events, who are exposed to salient policy changes, or whose local communities experience worsening policy outcomes.

Methodologically, I apply rigorous causal inference techniques to large-scale survey data and administrative databases while using survey experiments to test specific mechanisms and scope conditions. In order to understand the information environment around these events and policy outcomes, I apply natural language processing -- including classical methods, embedding-based models, and large language models -- to corpora of text including local newspaper articles.

My work has been published in [*Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2522718123){:target="_blank"}, [*PNAS Nexus*](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf185){:target="_blank"}, and [*Science Advances*](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abd8564){:target="_blank"}. I regularly collaborate with colleagues in public health, medicine, and public policy to study the intersection of health and politics. Past collaborations include the UCLA Health and Politics Project (2020 - 2024) and researchers at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. I am also a member of [the Practical Causal Inference Lab](https://www.practicallycausal.com){:target="_blank"}.

Prior to graduate school, I worked in the Office of the Medical Director at the [AIDS Institute](https://www.health.ny.gov/diseases/aids/){:target="_blank"} (New York State Department of Health) and on an analytics and data science team at [Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center](https://www.mskcc.org/){:target="_blank"}.
